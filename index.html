Clocks follow this device's time zone (local time). Date headings use that zone, so a race can fall on a different calendar date than the FIS date. Dates and start times come from the FIS World Cup calendars and database and are stored in UTC. Time TBA means FIS has not published a start time. Add to Google Calendar opens that race in Google Calendar, in a new tab, at the same UTC start. Download .ics stays on this page for Apple Calendar and uses the same UTC start. Live (FIS website) and Results (FIS website) open the FIS website in a new tab. Live shows from an hour before the start until the race is over, and all day on the venue's calendar day for a cross-country race with no start time. In that same window a live panel lists the leader, the last finisher, who is on course, the next three starters, and a top 10. It refreshes every 5 seconds during a run and every 60 seconds between runs, and it stops when the race is finished or this tab is hidden. If the timing relay does not answer, the panel stays hidden and Live still opens the FIS website. Finished races show the podium and stay under Today. Peacock, YouTube, and Outside TV open those sites in a new tab.
